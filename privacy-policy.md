# Fluff Privacy Policy

**Last updated: [DATE]**

Fluff is a place to slow down, so we've tried to keep this policy as calm and clear as the app itself. It explains what information Fluff uses, why, who helps us look after it, and the choices you have.

The short version: we collect very little. We don't sell your data, we don't show ads, we don't use analytics or tracking tools, and we don't ask about your health.

## What we collect, and why

### Your account

When you create a Fluff account, you sign in with **Apple** or **Google**. We don't ask for or store a password.

- **What we receive:** a unique account ID, your email address and the sign-in method you used. If you sign in with Apple and choose to share your name, Apple may also pass it to us. If you use Apple's "Hide My Email", we only receive a private relay address, not your real email.
- **Why:** to create and run your account, keep you signed in, and link your subscription to you so it works across your devices.
- **Lawful basis:** performance of our contract with you (providing the app you've asked for).

### Your subscription

Subscriptions are bought through the **Apple App Store** or **Google Play**. Apple or Google handle the payment. We never see or store your card or bank details.

- **What we receive:** whether your subscription is active, which plan you're on (monthly or yearly), its renewal or expiry date, and an anonymous purchase identifier. This is passed to us by RevenueCat, the service we use to check subscription status.
- **Why:** to unlock the full Fluff library and offline downloads, let you restore purchases, and help if something goes wrong with a subscription.
- **Lawful basis:** performance of our contract with you.

### When you contact us

- **What we receive:** your email address, the contents of your message, and anything you choose to include.
- **Why:** to reply and help you.
- **Lawful basis:** our legitimate interest in answering your questions and supporting our users.

### Technical information

Like any app that connects to the internet, Fluff's requests reach our service providers' servers, which briefly process technical information such as your IP address, device type and operating system version, and app version.

- **Why:** to deliver meditation audio, keep sign-in secure, protect our services from abuse, and fix problems.
- **Lawful basis:** our legitimate interest in running a secure and reliable app.

## What stays on your device

Some things never leave your phone:

- **Downloaded meditations.** Audio you download is saved on your device so you can listen offline. Audio is delivered from our content storage and isn't linked to your account.
- **Your daily reminder.** If you turn on a reminder, the time you choose is stored on your device and the reminder is scheduled by your phone's own notification system. We don't send it from a server, and we don't collect a push token.
- **Your sign-in session**, so you stay logged in.

You can remove downloads in the app, turn off reminders at any time in Fluff or your phone's settings, and remove everything stored on your device by deleting the app.

## What we don't do

- **We don't sell or rent your personal data**, and we don't share it for advertising.
- **We don't use analytics, advertising or cross-app tracking tools.** Fluff doesn't use your device's advertising identifier.
- **We don't collect health data.** Fluff doesn't ask about your mood, health or medical conditions, and we don't keep a record of which meditations you listen to on our servers.
- **We don't make automated decisions** about you that have legal or similarly significant effects.

## Who helps us run Fluff

We use a small number of trusted service providers. They act on our instructions ("processors") and may only use your data to provide their service to us.

| Provider | What they do for Fluff | Data involved |
|---|---|---|
| **Supabase** | Account sign-in and our database | Account ID, email, sign-in method, name if shared by Apple, subscription status |
| **RevenueCat** | Checks and manages subscription status across Apple and Google | Account ID, purchase and subscription records, device and app information |
| **Cloudflare** (R2 storage) | Stores and delivers meditation audio | Technical information such as IP address, needed to deliver files. No account information |
| **Apple (iCloud Mail)** | Our email inbox for hello@blankpagedigital.app | Emails you send us |

**Apple and Google** also play a part. When you sign in with Apple or Google, or buy a subscription through their stores, they handle that under their own privacy policies as independent controllers:

- Apple: [apple.com/legal/privacy](https://www.apple.com/legal/privacy/)
- Google: [policies.google.com/privacy](https://policies.google.com/privacy)

We may also share information if the law requires it, to protect the rights and safety of our users or others, or with a buyer if our business or Fluff is ever sold (in which case this policy would continue to protect your data).

## International transfers

Some of our providers are based in, or use servers in, countries outside the UK, including the United States. When your data is transferred outside the UK, we make sure it's protected to UK standards, for example by relying on UK "adequacy" regulations (including the UK Extension to the EU–US Data Privacy Framework, where the provider is certified) or by using the UK's International Data Transfer Agreement or Addendum to the EU Standard Contractual Clauses. You can email us for more detail about these safeguards.

- **Account and subscription information:** for as long as you have a Fluff account. When you delete your account, we delete it within [30] days. Copies in our providers' routine backups are removed automatically as those backups roll over, within a further [BACKUP PERIOD] days.

## Your rights

Under UK data protection law (the UK GDPR and the Data Protection Act 2018), you have the right to:

- **Access** the personal data we hold about you and get a copy.
- **Correct** anything that's inaccurate or incomplete.
- **Delete** your data ("the right to be forgotten").
- **Restrict** how we use your data in some circumstances.
- **Object** to us using your data where we rely on legitimate interests.
- **Data portability:** receive data you've given us in a commonly used, machine-readable format, or have it sent to another provider.
- **Withdraw consent** at any time, where we rely on consent (for example, turning off notification permission).

To use any of these rights, email **hello@blankpagedigital.app**. It's free, and we'll respond within one month. We may need to confirm it's really you, usually by asking you to write from the email address linked to your account.

You can delete your account at any time in the app (**Profile → Delete account**). See our [Account Deletion page](delete-account) for details.

## Children

Fluff is intended for people aged **13 and over**. We don't knowingly collect personal data from children under 13. If you believe a child under 13 has created an account, please email us and we'll delete it.

## Keeping your data safe

We use reputable providers, encrypted connections (HTTPS), access controls that mean each account can only read its own data, and we keep the data we hold to a minimum. No system is perfectly secure, but we take care to protect what you share with us.
